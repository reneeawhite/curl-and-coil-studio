# Source research checklist

Use this checklist before adding a provider, product, or care recommendation to the demo. The app should show a source link and a checked date for facts that can change.

## New Haven braiders and natural-style providers

For each provider, capture only public business information:

- Business or public professional name and New Haven neighborhood or public service address.
- Whether they explicitly offer box braids, knotless braids, twists, or twist-outs.
- Current service price or starting price, appointment length if listed, and whether washing and blow-drying are included or extra.
- Public rating platform, rating, review count, and listing URL. Keep Google and Apple ratings separate.
- Public booking, website, Instagram, or TikTok links.
- URL and date checked for each service and price detail.

Do not infer that a stylist serves textured hair from a photo alone. Do not publish a home address unless it is clearly listed as a public business location.

## Product candidates

Start research with Pattern, Cécred, As I Am, and Carol's Daughter. Add individual products only after checking a current official brand or retailer page.

For each product, capture:

- Exact product name, size, product category, and current listed price.
- Official product or retailer URL and date checked.
- Label ingredients or stated use only when the source provides them.
- The routine step and service where it may be relevant: braid preparation, braid maintenance, natural-style wash/styling, or takedown.
- A short matching reason tied to a user-selected need, hair attribute, or budget, with a source for any product claim.

Use the user's product budget to filter candidates. Do not claim a product is best for a curl type, prevents breakage, treats scalp conditions, or guarantees a result unless a trustworthy source supports the exact claim. Clearly label prices as checked-on dates because they can change.

## Care-plan sources

Prefer professional or educational sources for general hair-care guidance. For each instruction, store the source, the service and phase it applies to, the advice in plain language, and relevant limits or uncertainty. Keep general care guidance separate from brand marketing claims.

## First-pass size

For the deadline, target roughly 5–8 New Haven provider records and 8–12 product records across the two service paths. A smaller fully sourced catalog is better than a larger list with guessed prices or care claims.
